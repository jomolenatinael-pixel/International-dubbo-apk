## Grade 10 subject structure

The controlling document located is the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia’s *General Education Curriculum Framework*, dated December 2020. Its Table 4 lists **10 compulsory subjects** for Grades 9–10: **English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography, History, Citizenship Education, Economics, and Information Technology**. It then lists **five optional subjects, from which learners select two**: **First language, A Federal Language, Foreign language, Health and Physical Education, and Performing and Visual Arts**. The inspected PDF copy is hosted by EduMedia Depot, an archive host; the document identifies the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia, but the download is not from a Ministry domain. This is the framework’s structure; it does not prove that every option is offered in every region or school. [2020 framework, Table 4](https://edumedia-depot.gei.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/3a21f225-3730-4f7f-9d32-44b81cc7ef0b/content)

A separate Ministry-hosted *Curriculum Framework for Ethiopian Education (KG–Grade 12)* is dated May 2009. It reflects an older curriculum with a different subject and time structure; it should not be substituted for, or conflated with, the December 2020 Grades 9–10 table. [May 2009 Ministry framework](https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/Curriculum%20Framework%20for%20Ethiopian%20Education%20(KG%20%E2%80%93%20Grade%2012).pdf)

### Sample original learning card

- **Textbook-derived summary (paraphrase):** The Mathematics book covers polynomial functions, including how functions relate to their zeros. [Inspected Mathematics textbook](https://kehulum.com/bfile_asset/books_92/collection/grade-10-mathematics-new-curriculum--student-textbook-kehulumcom1759925836e39e.pdf)
- **Original learning objective:** Identify the zeros of a simple polynomial written in factored form and connect those zeros to its graph.
- **Original explanation:** In a product of factors, an input that makes one factor zero makes the whole product zero. For a real zero, the graph meets the horizontal axis at that input.

1. **Mathematics:** A polynomial function has zeros at −2 and 3 and a leading coefficient of 1. Write one possible factored form and explain how its zeros relate to the graph.
2. **Physics:** Sketch a simple circuit with two resistors in series and a battery. Describe where an ammeter and a voltmeter should be connected to measure current and potential difference.
3. **Biology:** In a food chain with plants, herbivores, and predators, explain how an ecological pyramid can represent energy transfer between trophic levels.
4. **Geography:** Choose two African climate zones and compare one way climate can affect human activity and one challenge associated with climate change.

## Safe use and limitations

For any learning app or derivative resource, use original explanations, summaries, and practice questions with citations; link learners to the original sources instead of bundling the PDFs. Request explicit permission or a license before incorporating rights-reserved textbook text, images, exercises, or other protected content. Online availability—including a government-hosted file, regional-bureau host, repository, or third-party mirror—does not grant redistribution rights. Where terms are unclear, keep the use to an attributed outline and original learning materials until rights are confirmed.

The supplied research reports **no subject-agent failures**. Important availability limits are recorded where they affect confidence: the Economics PDF was not inspected; no current optional language textbooks or contents were verified; the PVA full text was not accessible; and several new-curriculum textbooks were inspected only through third-party mirrors rather than an official host. These gaps are not treated as evidence that the books or subjects do not exist.
